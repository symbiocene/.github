Symbiocene builds ecological models you can question. Start with a place, change one assumption, and follow what happens to the water, sediment or plants. Each study names its sources and shows what the model assumes.

**[Run the Gowanus rainfall study](https://www.symbiocene.si/sims/gowanus/)** · [Explore a place](https://www.symbiocene.si/explore/) · [About Symbiocene](https://www.symbiocene.si/about/)

## Where the rain goes

Replay Hurricane Ida over a single Brooklyn block. Watch the water move from roofs and pavement toward the Gowanus Canal. Add green roofs or rain gardens, replay the storm, and compare how much runoff reaches the canal.

| Field study | What changes when you move a control? |
| --- | --- |
| [Sacramento Valley](https://www.symbiocene.si/sims/sacramento/) | Change the drawdown of a flooded rice field and watch its water depth and stored volume fall. |
| [Salish Sea](https://www.symbiocene.si/sims/salish-sea/) | Compare an armored shore with an unarmored one. Follow the sediment from the bluff along the beach. |
| [Magdalena River](https://www.symbiocene.si/sims/magdalena/) | Change the assumed survival rate in a riparian planting plan and count how many plants need replacing. |

## Atlases and public code

- **[Sphere](https://sphere.symbiocene.si/)** maps the infrastructure behind AI and the places where it is being built.
- **[Provider Graph](https://graph.symbiocene.si/)** lets you explore how environmental service providers relate to places and projects.
- **[Overshoot](https://github.com/starrybodies/overshoot)** is the public source for GAIA AI’s atlas of material flows. Follow a material journey or query the evidence directly.

[The Gaia archive](https://www.symbiocene.si/about/#gaia) traces the work that grew into Symbiocene.
